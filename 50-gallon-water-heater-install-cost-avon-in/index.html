What Drives the Cost of a 50-Gallon Water Heater Installed in Avon, IN

By the Avon 24/7 Plumbing team · Published October 30, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

Two quotes for a 50-gallon water heater can differ a lot, and the tank is rarely the reason. The difference usually sits in fuel type, venting, the condition of the existing connections, an expansion tank, permit work and what happens to the old unit.

We do not publish dollar figures here, because the real number depends on your setup. This guide shows what moves it and how to read an itemized written quote so you can compare two bids on the same job.

Five things that change the cost of the same 50-gallon tank

The size is the same, but the work around it is not. These are the factors that move the number most.

Fuel and heater type

A standard electric tank, a gas tank with a natural draft vent, a power-vent gas tank and a heat pump hybrid all hold 50 gallons and cost very different amounts to buy and install. Hybrid heat pump units also need airflow and a condensate drain.

Venting and gas connection

Swapping a gas heater into an existing chimney or vent is simpler than adding a new vent path through a wall or roof. Power-vent and direct-vent models need plastic venting runs, electrical power and, often, a condensate drain.

Condition of what is already there

Corroded shutoff valves, brittle flexible connectors and a leaking relief valve discharge line often need to be replaced while the heater is open. Old fittings that look fine can fail when disturbed.

Code items and permit

An expansion tank, a drain pan, a correctly piped temperature-and-pressure relief line and a permit for the work all add cost. Ask the Avon Planning and Building Department what applies to your job.

Access and disposal

A heater in a tight closet, an attic or a basement with steep stairs takes more time to remove and set. Hauling the old tank away is normally a line item of its own.

Homeowners policies typically respond to sudden and accidental water damage and typically exclude wear, gradual leaks and the failed heater itself, so ask your carrier whether a burst tank is covered before you decide on a repair or replacement.

How to read an itemized written quote

A good quote lists each part of the job separately. Use this table to check that nothing important is missing or hidden inside a single lump sum.

Why the quote that looks cheapest may not be the same job

A short quote may leave out the expansion tank, reuse old valves or skip the permit. Those gaps do not disappear. They become a surprise later, when a valve fails or an inspector asks for something that was never priced.

Ask each contractor to put the full scope on paper. If one quote lists a power-vent model and the other lists an atmospheric model, you are comparing different heaters, not different prices.

Getting a number you can trust

Follow this sequence and the quotes will be comparable.

  1. 1

    Write down what you have

    Note the fuel, the tank size, the age on the label and where the heater sits. A photo of the data plate helps.

  2. 2

    Ask whether the heater is being swapped or moved

    A like-for-like swap keeps the existing gas, venting and electrical. A move or fuel change means new work, and the quote should say so.

  3. 3

    Ask what is replaced and what is reused

    Valves, connectors and the relief discharge line are the usual extras. Each should be named.

  4. 4

    Confirm permit responsibility in writing

    Permit rules vary by job, so the quote should say who applies and who schedules the inspection.

    Compare totals only after the scope matches

Avon note: permits, hard water and who to call about gas

The Town of Avon Planning and Building Department handles permit applications online and can tell you what a water heater job requires, since permit rules vary by job. Its phone number is 317-272-0948.

Citizens Water reports system-wide hardness averaging about 19 grains per gallon, and it varies by treatment plant, so ask Citizens for the figure at your address. Hard water leaves scale on heating elements and the tank floor, which shortens a tank's life, so a descaling or flush habit and anode rod checks are worth a line in your decision.

If you smell gas near a gas water heater, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first. The Town lists CenterPoint Energy for gas service in Avon.

Source type: Town of Avon Building Department page, Local Utilities page and Citizens Energy Group water quality reports

What you can do yourself versus when to call

Water heater work mixes water, fuel and power, so the line between homeowner tasks and licensed work is clear.

  • You can compare quotes, take photos of the data plate and measure the closet before anyone arrives.
  • You can flush a tank and check for rust at the drain valve as maintenance.
  • Call for any gas connection, venting, relief line or new electrical circuit.
  • Call if there is water at the base of the tank, since a leaking tank cannot be repaired.
  • Call if the heater is in a spot you cannot reach safely, such as a tight attic.
  • Call before you move the unit, change fuel or swap to a heat pump model.

Codes, licensing and 811

Plumbing work in Indiana follows the state plumbing code (675 IAC 16), and the Indiana Plumbing Commission licenses plumbing contractors and journeyman plumbers. You can check any plumber through the license lookup at mylicense.in.gov before work starts, and you should call 811 before any digging.

Codes and standards decide what is allowed on a given job, so ask which ones apply to yours.
