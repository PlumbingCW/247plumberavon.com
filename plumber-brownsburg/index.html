Brownsburg's creek, its center and its growth

James B. Brown settled Brownsburg in 1824, the town was incorporated in 1848, and it was incorporated permanently in 1870. At the 2020 census it counted 28,973 residents, which makes it one of the larger towns in Hendricks County. A town that size has plumbing of many ages, so emergency dispatch around the clock has to cover old downtown buildings and newer houses alike.

White Lick Creek passes just west of the town center. Homes in low ground near any creek corridor can see groundwater and drainage trouble that homes on higher lots never meet. If water is coming in right now

Do these while you wait for the plumber, and skip any step that puts you near live electricity.

Shut off the supply

Turn the main valve clockwise at the point where the water line enters, or close the valve under the leaking fixture. If water keeps rising after that, the source is not the supply.

Mind the electrical

Never stand in water to reach a panel or a pump outlet. If the pump circuit is under water, leave it alone and tell the dispatcher.

Protect the low spots

Move boxes, furniture legs and electronics off the floor, and bail or squeegee toward a floor drain only if it is draining.

Smell gas? Leave

Gas odor from a furnace or water heater: walk everyone out and dial 911 or the gas utility from the yard or street. Call us once you are outside and safe.
