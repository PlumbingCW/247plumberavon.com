Locating Stonemill

The Town of Avon zoning index lists Stone Mill under R-2, a single-family residential class. Listing sites spell it Stonemill, and the listings show addresses such as Millbrook Drive. It is within Avon and the 46123 ZIP code.

We found no published boundary map, so Millbrook Drive is an anchor, not a boundary. If your street is close, call the line and ask.

Ranch houses from the late 1990s

The Stonemill listings we reviewed were single-family ranches built in 1997 to 1999, with three bedrooms, two bathrooms, two-car garages, about 1,100 to 1,700 square feet and lots of roughly a fifth to two fifths of an acre. Five listings is a sample, not a survey, but it paints a clear picture of a neighborhood of modest, single-level houses.

In a house of that size, all the plumbing shares a short run. That is convenient when something fails because the cause is rarely far from the symptom, and it also means one blocked main drain shows up in every bathroom and the kitchen almost at once.

What to do when a drain backs up

A short sequence that limits damage in a one-level house.

  1. 1

    Stop adding water

    Do not flush, run the dishwasher or start laundry. Every gallon has to go somewhere.

  2. 2

    Note which fixture is affected first

    The lowest or farthest fixture usually shows trouble first. That detail helps a plumber decide between a fixture clog and a main line problem.

  3. 3

    Keep chemicals out of it

    Drain products can burn skin and make the next step harder for a plumber. Avoid them if you plan to call.

  4. 4

    Call and describe it

    Ring (317) 740-1302. If several fixtures are involved, ask about sewer line cleaning. For a single sink or tub, drain cleaning is the usual start.

Utilities and hard water

Utility facts first. Avon lists Citizens Water for water and West Central Conservatory District for sewer. Citizens quotes about 19 grains per gallon as a system average with plant-to-plant variation, so check your own address. In a small house with short runs, scale tends to show up first at the faucet aerators and the water heater, and a softener is a common follow-up question.

Codes, licensing and 811

Plumbing work in Indiana follows the state plumbing code (675 IAC 16), and the Indiana Plumbing Commission licenses plumbing contractors and journeyman plumbers. You can check any plumber through the license lookup at mylicense.in.gov before work starts, and you should call 811 before any digging.

Codes and standards decide what is allowed on a given job, so ask which ones apply to yours.
