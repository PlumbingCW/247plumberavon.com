Using this site

This website gives general information about plumbing and about Avon 24/7 Plumbing. It is not a substitute for an on-site assessment by a licensed plumber. Content is provided as is, and plumbing conditions vary from home to home.

Services by phone

Services are arranged by phone at (317) 740-1302. A plumber will explain the scope and give a clear price before work starts. Scheduling depends on availability, and we do not promise a specific arrival time. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Emergencies

If you smell gas, see sparks near water, or believe anyone is in danger, leave the building and call 911 first. Our line is for plumbing dispatch and does not replace emergency services.

Licensing

Plumbing contractors and journeyman plumbers in Indiana are licensed by the Indiana Plumbing Commission. You can verify any plumber through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency license lookup at mylicense.in.gov.
