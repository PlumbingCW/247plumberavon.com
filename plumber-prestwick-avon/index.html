Two eras under one name

The Town of Avon zoning index lists Prestwick Estates, and Royal Troon appears there as its own entry. Listing sites also place The Parks at Prestwick here, a newer phase with new construction listings from a home builder. All of it falls inside Avon and the 46123 ZIP code.

Listings show addresses on Royal Troon Court, Cheltenham Way, Colchester Circle, Bracknell Drive, Amersham Court, Foxboro Drive and Parliament Way, plus East County Road 100 South nearby. No public boundary map turned up, so use those streets as anchors and call if your address is not on the list.

The older houses and the new ones

The older Prestwick listings we checked were single-family brick or frame houses built in 1993 and 1994, about 1,700 to 1,900 square feet, with lots near 0.25 to 0.43 acre and a monthly association fee on one of them. A recent listing in The Parks at Prestwick was a 2026 build of roughly 4,200 square feet on 0.48 acre. Two listings are an example, not a survey.

That spread matters for plumbing in a plain way. A house in its early thirties is the one where a worn water heater, a tired faucet or a slow main drain is a normal maintenance call. A house in its first years is more likely to need setup work, such as a softener, a recirculation fix or a fixture the owner wants changed.

Maintenance on houses from the 1990s

Water heaters, toilet internals and faucets are the usual wear items. A water heater replacement is easier to plan when the tank is not yet leaking, so look for rust at the fittings and a damp base.

Slow drains deserve attention before they stop. Drain cleaning clears the line, and a camera look can show whether the cause is buildup or something else.

Setup work on a newer house

Check your builder warranty before calling anyone, because some defects are the builder's to fix. Work outside that scope, such as a softener or a second fixture, is ordinary plumbing installation.

Hard water is a citywide topic here, and many owners of newer homes ask about water softener installation early.

Hot water choices

Both age groups ask about a tankless water heater when a tank is due or a larger household needs more hot water. The right answer depends on gas supply, venting and how many fixtures run at once.

Water and warranty facts worth knowing

Before sizing anything for hard water, get the number. Citizens Water, the provider the Town of Avon lists, reports an average of roughly 19 grains per gallon across its system and a wide spread between plants, so the figure at a Prestwick address may differ. Once you have it, a water softener can be sized to match.

On a new home, keep the builder paperwork and the water heater label together. That record saves time when a plumber needs to know what is installed.

A note on boundaries

Codes, licensing and 811

Plumbing work in Indiana follows the state plumbing code (675 IAC 16), and the Indiana Plumbing Commission licenses plumbing contractors and journeyman plumbers. You can check any plumber through the license lookup at mylicense.in.gov before work starts, and you should call 811 before any digging.

Codes and standards decide what is allowed on a given job, so ask which ones apply to yours.
