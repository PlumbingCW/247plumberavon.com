Water, scale and the service line

Avon's utilities page names Citizens Water as the water provider and West Central Conservatory District for sewer. Citizens publishes system-wide hardness of about 19 grains per gallon, but the number differs by treatment plant, so request the figure for your own address. Scale from hard water builds on heating elements and aerators over many years, and a softener is one answer owners ask about.

Citizens also says the service line from the main to the building belongs to the property owner. If you plan work near the street side of your lot, call 811 before anyone digs. Sewer and water questions at a specific address are best confirmed with the utilities themselves.

Living near the water

Many Austin Lakes lots sit close to a pond or lake. These habits help any home on a lot like that.

Know the main shutoff

Find it now, and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. A frozen or failed supply line is far less costly when the water is off within a minute.

Test the sump pump

If your house has a pit and pump, pour in a bucket of water each season and watch it start and empty. A failed pump is covered under sump pump repair.

Aim downspouts and drains away

Roof water that pools against the foundation ends up where you do not want it. This is a drainage habit, not a claim that any particular lot floods.

Photograph the label on your heater

The date and model on the tank tell a plumber what you own before they arrive, which speeds up a water heater replacement decision.

Call the line right away if

Any one of these is worth a call at any hour.

  • Water is running and you cannot stop it at the fixture or the main
  • Wastewater is coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain
  • The water heater is leaking from the tank or smells of gas or burning
  • You hear water running when every fixture is off
  • A ceiling stain is spreading or dripping

Codes, licensing and 811

Plumbing work in Indiana follows the state plumbing code (675 IAC 16), and the Indiana Plumbing Commission licenses plumbing contractors and journeyman plumbers. You can check any plumber through the license lookup at mylicense.in.gov before work starts, and you should call 811 before any digging.

Codes and standards decide what is allowed on a given job, so ask which ones apply to yours.
