Why the age of a Danville house changes the call

Danville was founded in 1824, incorporated as a town in 1835 and is the Hendricks County seat. Two National Register historic districts, Courthouse Square and Main Street, mark the old center. Older housing near a historic downtown usually means older pipe somewhere in the system, and that is a general inference rather than a record of any specific street.

Avon sits about 7 miles east and grew from 6,248 residents in 2000 to 21,474 in 2020, so much of it was built after 2000. How an older-pipe emergency is handled

The order matters because brittle pipe can crack when forced.

  1. 1

    Stop the water gently

    Close the main valve slowly. An old gate valve may stick or leak at the stem, and forcing it can make things worse, so stop and tell us if it will not turn.

  2. 2

    Say what you can see

    Tell the dispatcher the pipe color, any rust stains and where the water is spreading. Those details tell us which tools to load.

  3. 3

    Find the break without opening everything

    Acoustic listening and a pressure test narrow a hidden leak, and a camera does the same for a drain. Leak detection keeps the wall cuts small.

  4. 4

    Choose the repair

    A single failed section can be replaced alone. If the pipe is failing in several places, repiping becomes a fair discussion, and the decision is yours.

Before we arrive in Danville

A few moves protect an older house while help is on the way.

Ease the main closed

Turn the valve clockwise slowly until the flow stops. If it is seized or weeping at the handle, leave it and tell the dispatcher.

Check the panel floor

Before switching breakers, confirm the floor at the panel is dry. In many older houses, basement panels sit low.

Catch and clear

Place buckets and towels under the active drip and lift belongings from low shelves. Older ceilings can sag, so stay out from under bulging plaster.

Gas or sewage

At any gas odor, evacuate and let 911 or the gas utility deal with it before anything else. If sewage is backing up through old drains, stop using fixtures until we arrive.
