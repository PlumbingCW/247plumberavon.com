Who we are

Avon 24/7 Plumbing operates from 5151 E US Highway 36, Avon, IN 46123. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit 247plumberavon.com or call (317) 740-1302.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or comment boxes, so we do not collect information you type into the site. Your browser sends standard technical data to our host and to Google Fonts and Google Maps when those load, such as your IP address and device type. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use call information

When you call, we use the details you give us, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem, to dispatch a plumber and to follow up on the job. We do not sell call recordings or contact details.

Cookies and third parties

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. Embedded maps and fonts come from Google and are governed by Google's own policies. If you prefer not to load them, you can block third party content in your browser and the rest of the site will still work.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@247plumberavon.com or calling (317) 740-1302. We will respond as required by Indiana and federal law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date below. Last updated: October 30, 2026.
